About Us

Founder

Hajara Bello is a dedicated and experienced instructor of Islamic studies, passionate about nurturing a love for learning in students of all ages. With 5 years of experience in teaching, she holds a Degree in Politicial Science, a Masters in Psychology and an Islamic studies certification from Rabi'at Academic Foundation and has studied under various scholars. She is committed to providing personalized instruction in Quran, Tajweed, Hifdh, Fiqh, Seerah, Islamic history e.t.c, in a supportive and engaging environment. Her goal is to empower students with a strong foundation in Islamic knowledge and values.

She believes in fostering a deep understanding of the Quran through interactive lessons and practical application.

She is passionate about sharing her knowledge and helping students connect with their faith.



Our Approach

We believe that learning should be both enriching and enjoyable. Our teachers employ a student-centered, interactive, personalized approach, tailoring thier lessons to meet the individual needs and learning styles of each student. They are dedicated to creating a positive and encouraging learning environment where students feel comfortable asking questions and exploring their faith.



Our Values

Integrity, respect, compassion, pursuit of knowledge. We strive to instill these values in our students, helping them to become not only knowledgeable but also compassionate and responsible members of the community.




Courses Offered




Quran Recitation

Focuses on correct pronunciation, fluency, and memorization techniques. Suitable for beginners and advanced learners.

Level: All Levels

Duration: Flexible



Tajweed

Master the rules of Tajweed for beautiful and accurate Quranic recitation. Learn proper articulation and intonation.

Level: All Levels

Duration: Flexible



Hisnul Muslim

Study the comprehensive collection of supplications (duas) from the Quran and Sunnah for daily life.

Level: Intermediate/Advanced

Duration: Flexible



Islamic History

Explore the rich history of Islam, from the life of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) and other prophets to the life of the pious predecessors.

Level: All Levels

Duration: Flexible



Hadith Studies

Learn about the importance of Hadith, its compilation, and its role in Islamic jurisprudence. Focus on understanding and application.

Level: Intermediate/Advanced

Duration: Flexible
